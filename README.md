# Foraged Florals

A quasi-educational website dedicated to foraging and preparing edible flowers.

## About

Foraged Florals is a beginner guide to discovering, harvesting, and cooking with edible flowers. Learn about safe foraging practices, proper flower identification, preparation techniques, and delicious recipes featuring edible flowers like magnolias, dandelions, marigolds, and chive blossoms.

## Features

- **Educational Content** — Learn about edible flowers and safe foraging practices
- **Recipe Collection** — Try featured recipes like Magnolia Snap Cookies and Fried Dandelions
- **Responsive Design** — Works seamlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices
- **Beautiful Imagery** — High-quality photos of flowers and prepared dishes
- **Contact Form** — Reach out to submit your own recipes or ask questions (please note this does not fully function)

## Pages

- **Home** (`index.html`) — Introduction to foraging, preparing, and cooking with edible flowers
- **Flowers** (`flowers.html`) — Detailed guide to edible flower varieties
- **Recipes** (`recipes.html`) — Full recipes with ingredients and instructions
- **About Us** (`aboutus.html`) — Contact form and resource links

## Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser to view the site
2. Navigate using the menu bar to explore different pages
3. Fill out the contact form on the About Us page to get in touch
